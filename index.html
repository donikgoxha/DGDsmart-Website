Repair catalog

What we fix.

  • Screen & hinge replacement

    Cracked, flickering or dead laptop displays, plus broken hinges and loose lids.

  • Battery & charging issues

    Batteries that die in minutes, chargers that no longer charge, DC jack replacement.

  • Overheating & cleaning

    Thermal paste renewal, fan and heatsink cleaning, for machines that throttle.

  • SSD, RAM & storage upgrades

    Turn a slow machine into a fast one, with your data migrated across.

  • Windows & software repair

    Boot failures, blue screens, driver problems, virus removal, clean OS reinstall.

  • Motherboard & liquid damage

    Board-level diagnostics and component work on machines that will not power on.

  • Data recovery & backup

    Files rescued from failing drives, and a backup set up so it does not repeat.

  • Keyboard & trackpad

    Dead keys, spilled drinks, unresponsive trackpads and worn palm rests.

  • Screen & glass replacement

    Cracked glass, dead touch, lines across the display — phones and tablets.

  • Battery replacement

    Fast-draining or swollen batteries swapped for fresh cells.

  • Charging port repair

    Ports that only charge at an angle, or stopped charging altogether.

  • Camera, speaker & mic

    Blurry cameras, silent speakers, callers who cannot hear you.

  • Water damage treatment

    Board cleaning and corrosion treatment after a spill or a swim.

  • Software & data transfer

    Stuck updates, boot loops, and moving everything to a new device.

  • Smartwatches & wearables

    Screens, batteries and straps for watches and fitness trackers.

  • Headphones & speakers

    Charging cases, cable breaks and audio that only plays on one side.

  • Game consoles

    Overheating, HDMI ports, drives and controllers that drift.

  • Printers & peripherals

    Monitors, printers, routers and everything else plugged into your desk.

  • Power supplies & adapters

    Chargers and PSUs tested, repaired or matched with a correct replacement.

  • Not sure what it is?

    Bring it in. If it has a circuit board, we will take a look at it.

Price depends on the model and the parts involved, so we quote after diagnostics — always before any work begins. Send us the details and we will come back with a figure.
