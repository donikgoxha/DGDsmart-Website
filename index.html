FAQ

Common Questions

The things people ask us most before bringing a device in.

Do I need an appointment for a repair?

No. You can walk into the shop at JNA 71 during working hours. Sending the repair form or a message first just means we can prepare and answer you faster.

How long does a repair take?

It depends on the fault and on parts availability. Simple jobs are often done quickly, while board-level work or ordered parts take longer. You get a timeframe together with the quote, before we start.

How much will it cost?

We quote after diagnostics, because the same symptom can have very different causes. The price is agreed with you before any work begins — there are no surprise charges at pickup.

Will I lose my data?

We work to preserve your data, and we tell you in advance if a repair carries any risk to it. Where a drive is already failing, we offer data recovery. Backing up before you hand the device over is always the safest move.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Repair work and the parts we fit are covered by warranty. If the same fault returns within that period, bring the device back to us.

Can you build a PC to my budget?

That is one of our favourite jobs. Tell us what you will use it for — gaming, office, editing, study — and your budget, and we will put together a build, assemble it and set it up.

Do you repair devices you did not sell?

Yes. It does not matter where the device came from. Laptops, desktops, phones, tablets, consoles and most consumer electronics are welcome.

What if the device cannot be fixed?

We tell you honestly, explain why, and where it makes sense we suggest a replacement or a cheaper alternative rather than spending money on a lost cause.
